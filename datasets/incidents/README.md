# Public Incident Case Studies

`public_case_studies.json` is a small hand-curated learning index from official U.S. Chemical Safety and Hazard Investigation Board (CSB) investigation pages. Each summary links to its primary source. These are public case studies for retrieval and training prompts, not ATLAS site incidents.

The cases are not model labels. Public investigations describe outcomes and contributing factors but do not provide synchronized plant sensor, permit, maintenance, or pre-incident time-series records. Do not join these records to the simulator or use them to claim predictive performance.

The CSB says its Accidental Release Reporting Rule data covers incidents reported under the rule since March 23, 2020, is revised quarterly, and may contain corrections or late reports. Its separately compiled incident-news reports may be incomplete and are not independently verified; CSB cautions against statistical conclusions from those reports. The rule data is consequence-selected (fatality, serious injury, or substantial property damage), so it is not a representative negative/positive sample for early-warning model training.
